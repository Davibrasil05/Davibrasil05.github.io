Gridoo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Gridoo is a block puzzle game for iPhone made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. In short: your game progress stays on your iPhone, and the only data that leaves it goes to Google (ads), Apple (Game Center and purchases).

Data that stays on your device

Your saved run, records, collection, settings and run history are stored only on your iPhone. We do not have a server and never receive this data. The "Export runs" option in Options creates a spreadsheet that you can choose to share; nothing is sent unless you share it yourself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gridoo is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between runs and an optional rewarded ad for extra plays. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information, an approximate location based on your IP address, ad interactions and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • On iPhone, you choose whether to allow tracking in the Apple prompt. The game is the same either way; without permission, ads are not personalized.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form lets you accept, refuse or manage how your data is used.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Remove ads purchase

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the game only checks with Apple whether the purchase was made.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score in the daily challenge and quick run is sent to Apple's leaderboards together with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

Gridoo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.
  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop seeing full-screen ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
